Ecosystem

Each app feeds the others

The Nexrive vision is a single account that gathers what today lives scattered across notebooks, chats and general social networks.

  1. Volta to Avanti

    Maintenance logged in Volta backs the vehicle profile in the community with a verifiable history.

  2. Avanti to Meetings

    From a build profile you can call or join a meet with the people who already follow it.

  3. Meetings to Volta

    What happens at an event returns to the vehicle record: the trip, the kilometers and the photos of the day.

These connections describe the product direction and will go live as Avanti and Meetings are released.

Principles

How we build

The car is the star

Our products revolve around the vehicle and its story, not the popularity of whoever drives it.

Useful from day one

Each app must solve a real problem on its own, even offline and without depending on the others.

History that adds value

A tidy, verifiable record makes the vehicle easier to maintain and backs it when it is time to sell.

Community before numbers

We prefer a small group of passionate people over a large number of unused downloads.
